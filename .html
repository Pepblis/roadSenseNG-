Road Safety Tips

🚗 Avoid Speeding

Speed gives you less time to react to unexpected situations. Drive at a safe and appropriate speed.

📱 Don't Drive Distracted

Keep your attention on the road and avoid using your phone while driving.

🚦 Obey Traffic Signs

Traffic signs and signals are there to protect everyone using the road.

Make Safety a Habit

Every safe decision on the road can make a difference.
